Controls

  • W A S D move, or ← → turn
  • Mouse: look around (click the game to capture the mouse)
  • Left click or Ctrl fire, Space or E use / open doors
  • Shift run, Alt strafe, 1-7 weapons
  • Esc menu (press Esc once to release the mouse first), Tab automap

Sound effects and music are not included in this build yet (see README). Game data: Freedoom (BSD-3-Clause). Engine: doomgeneric (GPL-2.0).
